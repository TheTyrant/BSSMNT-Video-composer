Render speed test

Measures how fast this machine can do the offline renderer's work at the chosen size: decode a real clip, draw each visual mode with the app's own drawing code, encode to H.264, and the full clip pipeline (decode → composite → encode). Nothing leaves this computer. “× real time” is how many seconds of video one second of rendering produces at 30 fps.
